1. The Basics: Scope vs. Flat on Your Screen
  • Scope (2.39:1 — Wide Format): About 95% of movies come in this wide format. Because your flat screen is built tall, playing a Scope movie leaves big black bars at the top and bottom.
  • Flat (1.85:1 — Tall Format): This taller format fills 100% of your flat screen from top to bottom with zero black bars, giving audiences a massive, full-screen picture.
❌ Scope (2.39:1) on Your Flat Screen
Leaves thick black bars on top & bottom of your tall screen.
✅ Flat (1.85:1) on Your Flat Screen
Fills 100% of your entire screen height & width.
2. The Catch with Avengers: Doomsday ("Infinity Vision")

Marvel is releasing Avengers: Doomsday in two DCP versions: a standard Scope (2.39:1) version and an Expanded Flat (1.85:1) version. Both versions are also offered under Marvel's "Infinity Vision" certification depending on a cinema's screen shape:

  • If you DO NOT apply for Infinity Vision: Disney will only send you the standard Scope (2.39:1) DCP. Your tall flat screen will be forced to play the movie with big black bars on top and bottom.
  • If you APPLY for Infinity Vision: Because you have a tall flat screen, Disney will approve and send you the exclusive 1.85:1 Expanded Flat DCP, filling your entire screen.

3. Your Screen Already Meets All Marvel Requirements

To get Infinity Vision certification from Disney, an auditorium only needs:

  • Screen Width: At least 45 feet wide
  • Brightness: Standard 14 fL (2D) / 6 fL (3D)
  • Audio: 7.1 Surround or Dolby Atmos
  • Flat Screen: Qualifies for the 1.85:1 Flat DCP

Your auditorium already meets every single requirement—no new equipment or hardware changes are needed.

4. Why This Will Bring Huge Crowds to Your Cinema

Very few cinemas in a massive geographical radius have a 45+ ft tall flat screen. If you get certified and play the 1.85:1 Flat version, fans from neighboring cities and towns will travel long distances specifically to your cinema to watch Avengers: Doomsday full-screen.

What You Need to Do:
  1. Apply to Disney/Marvel Distribution Now: Request Infinity Vision certification for your flat screen well ahead of release.
  2. Request the 1.85:1 Flat DCP: Once approved, ensure your programming and projection team specifically orders and plays the 1.85:1 Flat version (not the Scope version) on your flat screen.
